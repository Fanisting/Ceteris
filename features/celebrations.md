# Celebrations

When an agent finishes while you are looking at another tab, three small particles fly off the done dot and fade in under a second.

The style follows your theme. Cute themes use hearts, Rose Pine and Meizi use petals, pearl themes use a ring of light, and Business and Windows Terminal show a plain check mark. Other themes use stars.

You can also turn on a small pebble in the title bar. It naps when all agents are idle, opens its eyes while an agent works, and smiles when one finishes.

Both options are in Settings under Appearance. The pebble is off by default. Nothing makes a sound or takes focus, and nothing plays if your system asks for reduced motion.
