# Legado MD3 themes

The Legado MD3 series brings 11 Material 3 colour sets from the Android reader app legado-with-MD3. Each has a light and a dark version.

There are also two live modes, Custom and Windows Accent. Pick one colour, or follow your Windows accent colour, and Ceteris builds a full palette from it. The terminal colours change along with the window. A change to the Windows accent applies right away.

You can choose among nine palette styles and three contrast levels. Find them in Settings under Appearance, in the theme menu.
