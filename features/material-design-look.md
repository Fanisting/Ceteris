# Material Design 3 look

Windows, menus, dialogs and settings follow Material Design 3. The window is one shell with a rounded content card. The sidebar and settings navigation use pill-shaped active items, and switches, text fields and dialogs have the Material shapes.

Motion is Material too. Dialogs and notices fade out, context menus grow from where you click, and new content fades in when you switch settings pages or open a file. Motion never touches the terminal and never delays its focus.

If your system asks for reduced motion, all animation is off.
