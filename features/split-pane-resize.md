# Resize split panes

The buttons at the bottom right of the main area split it into two, three or four terminals. The line between two panes can be dragged.

Point at the gap between two terminals and the cursor turns into a resize arrow; a thin coloured line shows which divider you hold. Drag it to give one terminal more room. A double click puts the line back in the middle.

In the layouts with one big pane and two small ones, the short line between the two small panes moves only those two. In the four-pane grid, the two lines move all four panes together.

Each side keeps at least 120 pixels. The terminals refit to their new size as soon as you pause, and the program inside sees the new width and height.

The sizes belong to the window and last as long as it is open, like the layout itself. Changing the layout keeps them; a new window starts with even halves. There is no keyboard shortcut for this.
